# Attributed public weather facts

NOAA NODD permits attributed use of these public data. Publish selected station/time/temperature facts, source hashes and original derivative analysis, without NOAA endorsement or claiming a derivative is unchanged agency output. Full source files, technical publications and artwork remain outside Git. Airport identifiers/metadata describe public infrastructure; no personal/passenger/clinical/product records are used. Missingness and unavailable Lite quality flags remain explicit limitations. Formal-publication projects remain private.
