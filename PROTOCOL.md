## Observation and quality semantics

## Fixed thresholds and fair aggregation comparison

These are untuned illustrative outdoor planning scenarios, not manufacturer limits, food/drug safety thresholds, validated operating setpoints or optimized alert cutoffs. Retain equality-at-threshold as nonexceeding. All numerical comparisons use integer tenths and exact rational means.

For each station/year/UTCmonth and threshold, let n be observed temperature slots, s their integer-tenths sum and e the number with T>10u. A monthly-mean view labels all n observed slots positive iff s>10u*n. Compare that coarsened view against the same observed near-hour readings. The observed target is reported temperature, not true product state. No probability calibration is applicable; no predictive classification claim.

The monthly view's positive-slot count is n*I(mean>u); the reading-level positive count is e. A mean below the threshold does not imply no individual reading exceeded it.
